Reggae, ska, punk fusion bringing big brass, big bounce, and high energy festival vibes from Bedfordshire, UK.

easydread blend reggae groove, ska energy, and punk attitude into a colourful, high-tempo live sound rooted in UK festival culture. Their music is built for movement: upbeat guitars, bouncing horn lines, dub-infused breakdowns, and choruses that pull whole crowds in.

Sound of the Uprising is easydread's latest burst of reggae warmth, ska-bright brass, and punk-charged energy, capturing the feelgood chaos of a band built for festival stages. The record celebrates togetherness, movement, and real-life stories, blending bouncing horn lines, dub-soaked textures, and sing-ready hooks into a soundtrack for anyone who's ever found joy in a crowd.
