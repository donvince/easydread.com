Reggae, ska & punk fusion bringing big brass, big bounce and high-energy festival vibes from Bedfordshire, UK.

Band Overview

easydread blend reggae groove, ska energy, and punk attitude into a colourful, high-tempo live sound rooted in UK festival culture. Their music is built for movement: upbeat guitars, bouncing horn lines, dub-infused breakdowns, and choruses that pull whole crowds in.

Their music celebrates togetherness, community, and the joyfully chaotic spirit of live performance - the sound of people moving as one.

Latest Release

Album Sound of the Uprising is easydread's latest burst of reggae warmth, ska-bright brass, and punk-charged energy, capturing the feelgood chaos of a band built for festival stages.

The record blends bouncing horn lines, dub-soaked textures and sing-ready hooks into a soundtrack for anyone who's ever found joy in a crowd.
